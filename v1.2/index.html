About

A type tool for every script.

type_space is an open, browser-based font remixer for graphic and type designers. It turns the slow parts of extending a typeface — weights, widths, matching a Latin to an Indic or African script, harmonising proportions — into precise, reversible operations on real outlines, and exports installable fonts with Unicode mapping and OpenType shaping intact.

Many writing systems are encoded in Unicode but have one typeface, sometimes none. Tirhuta, Modi, Lepcha, Bamum, Wancho and dozens more deserve a range of voices. type_space starts from the open fonts that exist and helps designers grow them into families.

How it works

  1. Read. Fonts are parsed table by table in your browser: exact TrueType points, CFF converted to quadratics, variable fonts instanced from gvar.
  2. Measure. Stems, bars, contrast, stress, zones and headlines are measured from the outlines for every script the font covers.
  3. Draft. Every change is previewed instantly with a point-preserving engine — the same outlines drive the morph animation.
  4. Refine. A stroke-aware engine then rebuilds each glyph as a filled region: overlaps merged, the letter's core transformed, the stroke regrown with an elliptical pen, thinning proportional to local thickness, and curves refitted with nodes on extrema. No spikes, folds or snapped joins.
  5. Compile. A TrueType font is written with your new names; GSUB and other layout tables are carried over, and GPOS anchors and kerning are moved with the outlines.
  6. Learn. Your exports and the settings you keep train an on-device model that predicts how you harmonise scripts and what you typically do with each class of type.

Privacy

Your fonts stay with you.

  • Fonts you upload are processed entirely in your browser. They are never sent to a server.
  • Library fonts are downloaded directly from the public Google Fonts repository on GitHub.
  • Your project, learning data and API key are stored in this browser (IndexedDB and localStorage). Clearing site data removes them.
  • The type assistant is optional. When you use it, the measured analysis of your font (numbers, not the font file), your request and — for image transfer — your image are sent from your browser to Anthropic using your own API key.
  • There is no analytics, no tracking and no account.

Fonts keep their own licenses. Open Font License fonts can be modified and redistributed under the OFL with a new family name.

AI & Learning

type_space learns from how you work. Every export and every setting you settle on becomes a training example for an on-device model that predicts how you like scripts harmonised and which moves you make for each class of type.

Type assistant (Claude)

The assistant reads the measured analysis of your font and returns parameter settings with typographic reasoning. Your key is stored only in this browser and requests go directly from your browser to Anthropic.
